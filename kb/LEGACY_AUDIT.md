# Legacy corpus audit (`kb_documents`)

`kb_documents` was loaded before the source registry existed. It holds two
kinds of rows, checked against the admission policy in
`docs/adr/0006-knowledge-base-sources.md`.

| Rows | Origin | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| `kb_*` (26) | Articles written by the PredictiX team (also in `knowledge_base`) | **Served.** Own content; no licence issue. |
| `pdf_*` (1,291) | Ten PDFs loaded from a local folder; only file names were recorded | **Not served** (since 2026-11-24) until each is re-admitted through `kb/sources.yaml` |

Retrieval excludes the `pdf_*` rows unless `KB_LEGACY_PDFS_ENABLED=true`
(`app/kb/kb_vector_store.py`). The rows are kept, not deleted, so the
decision can be reversed.

URL or internal location, licence and evidence, and run the pipeline. It then
gets provenance, scoping and citations like every other source.
